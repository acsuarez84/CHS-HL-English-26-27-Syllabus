Name Period

Six tabs. Grades, late work and submission are on tab five — the one most people are looking for.

Attendance & Course Expectations

Attendance

The attendance policy in place aims to create a more engaging and accomplished school year. Please refer to the Parent/Student Handbook.

Behaviour

This course has frequent collaborative activities and debates. Students are highly encouraged to bring their own perspectives to a topic — and to do it respectfully. Everyone is treated equally and everyone takes part.

Class presentations

Not everyone likes speaking in front of others, but this course has many oral components, so short presentations are required. Accommodations will be given as needed. You will always be given time to prepare and a clear picture of what is expected. Students growing in English as a second language are encouraged to use their native language where applicable.

Classwork & readings

Classwork will include:

  • Research practice
  • Heavy genre analyses
  • Thesis and essay structure outlining
  • Reflection responses through argumentation and synthesis

Assignments are to be completed as set.

Difference of Opinion About Content

Parents and students who are unsure about the nature of the content in an assignment or a reading are welcome to have a conversation with me. Not every topic is interpreted the same way by everyone, and feedback is always welcome.

Please note that there is a state and district curriculum we must follow, and all readings are state-approved.

To parents. Teaching is not a solo profession — it is a partnership with you. Please help us hold the line on the cell phone rules and on homework completion. If you need help with resources, contact me or the school administration. We are here to help.

Non-Negotiable Rules

Cell phones

Florida has a strict policy on cell phones, smart watches and earbuds. Unless there is a medical need or the teacher has given permission for educational use, devices go in book bags, silenced or switched off. Unauthorised use of any listed device results in a referral.

Backpacks

Backpacks go on top of or below the wooden table on the right side of the classroom.

Snacking

Only students with a medical need to eat during class may do so. If that applies to you, take the snack out on entering and place it on your desk — it prevents disruption mid-activity. Everyone else: water only.

Cheating & Plagiarism

Any cheating or plagiarism results in a report to the IB coordinator, a parent meeting, and a zero on the assignment with no remediation. It is not worth it.

See Grades & late work for the grading scale and the late penalties.

Turnitin

This course uses turnitin.com, which compares each submission against billions of web pages and a database of student papers that grows with every submission. You may be expected to submit assignments electronically. After processing, I receive a report stating if and how another author’s work was used.

What is assessed

Assessments make up 100% of the grade.

  • Presentations — 100 pts
  • Written assignments — 100 pts
  • Student Profile Portfolio — 100 pts

Submitting work

All assignments are submitted on the posted due date via Canvas, unless another medium is specified. Canvas is the main source for every lesson and assignment; if another forum is used you will be told immediately. Focus remains the gradebook, and I add annotations there as a way of keeping parents informed.

Late work

One day late: 10% off the total grade.
Each further day: another 10% off.
Major assignments more than 5 days late: returned with full feedback, but 0% of the total grade.

Do not be late.

Absences

After an excused absence within the guidelines in the Parent/Student Handbook, you are responsible for completing the work you missed. It should be submitted one day after the date of your absence. Check Canvas and Focus first.

Completion grades

Completion grades are not remediated and do not follow the late policy — full credit is given when the work is done. Only parental notification through ParentSquare allows a completion assignment to be submitted without penalty.

Artificial Intelligence

Osceola School District strictly prohibits the general use of AI. In this course, you will be taught to use it responsibly — and AI itself forms part of the science, technology and the environment global issue we examine across the year.

Use of AI only with acknowledgement

You may use AI tools on assignments if the use is properly documented and credited. Any attempt to pass AI output off as your own work is plagiarism and will be treated as such.

How to cite it. Text generated by Copilot should carry a citation such as:
“Copilot. Accessed 2023-12-03. Prompt: ‘Summarize the Geneva Convention in 50 words.’ Generated using Copilot-Web”
