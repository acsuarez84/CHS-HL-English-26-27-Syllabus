Section 8

T. S. Eliot — Selected Poems

Nineteen poems, studied in Year 2 Quarter 1 over roughly two weeks. Wikisource and Poetry Foundation links are stable and classroom-friendly.

Concepts: Identity, Transformation, Representation. Global issue: Beliefs, values and education — spiritual crisis, modernity versus tradition, the secular and the sacred.
