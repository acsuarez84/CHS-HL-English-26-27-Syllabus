Section 7

Diploma Programme Assessments (HL)

The four assessed components, what each one actually rewards, and when it falls.

IB Language & Literature · HL

Assessments

2 external papers · 2 internal tasks
Name Period

One tab per component. Each explains what the examiner is looking for, not just what the task is.

Paper 1 — Guided Textual Analysis

Two guided analyses of previously unseen non-literary extracts.

You are given texts you have never seen and a guiding question for each. The examiner is testing whether you can read a text type on sight — recognise what it is, who it is for, and how its form carries its argument.

Practise with any of the ten bodies of work; the text-type question bank is built for this.

When: Timed, in school, on paper — May 2028

Paper 2 — Comparative Essay

Four general questions; you write on two works studied.

Neither work may already have been used in another assessment. The commonest weakness is alternating paragraphs instead of genuinely comparing. Find the problem two works share, then show how each solves it differently.

Othello and The Hour of the Star both ask who is permitted to narrate a life — and answer in opposite directions.

When: Timed, in school, on paper — May 2028

Individual Oral — Internal Assessment

10 minutes prepared, then 5 minutes of questions.

Supported by one extract from a non-literary text and one from a literary work, which must share a global issue. You open by naming your field of inquiry, then narrow to your issue — the move practised all year on the concepts page and in the ladder.

Internally assessed, externally moderated by the IB.

When: Mid-March to mid-April 2027. The deadline is non-negotiable

HL Essay — 1,200–1,500 words

A line of inquiry of your own, on one studied text or work.

The one task you build slowly. A line of inquiry is a question you do not yet know the answer to — open enough to argue, narrow enough to finish. If you can answer it in a sentence, it is a topic, not a line of inquiry.

See the worked examples on each field tab of the concepts page.

When: Drafted over the summer and first half of senior year; submitted before Winter Break, December 2027
