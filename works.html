Penguin Modern ClassicsThe TunnelSabato · Novella · Argentina, 1956
APASabato, E. (2011). The tunnel (M. S. Peden, Trans.). Penguin Classics. (Original work published 1948)
MLASabato, Ernesto. The Tunnel. Translated by Margaret Sayers Peden, Penguin Classics, 2011.
  • Identity — Castel’s fractured, narcissistic self; obsessive consciousness.
  • Perspective — Unreliable first-person confession; subjective reality.
  • Communication — The failure of authentic human connection.
 Culture, identity and community

Alienation and isolation in modern urban society; the impossibility of being truly known.

Also: Beliefs, values and education (existentialist worldview)
New Directions, trans. Benjamin MoserThe Hour of the StarLispector · Novella · Brazil, 1977
APALispector, C. (2011). The hour of the star (B. Moser, Trans.). New Directions. (Original work published 1977)
MLALispector, Clarice. The Hour of the Star. Translated by Benjamin Moser, New Directions, 2011.
  • Identity — Macabéa’s invisible existence; the narrator’s metafictional self.
  • Representation — How the marginalised poor are represented; authorial ethics.
  • Creativity — Metafiction; the act of writing as creation and exposure.
 Culture, identity and community

Class, gender, and regional identity (Northeast Brazil → Rio); the invisibility of poverty.

Also: Politics, power and justice (gendered and economic power)
Folger Shakespeare LibraryOthelloShakespeare · Tragedy · UK, 1622
APAShakespeare, W. (2017). Othello (B. A. Mowat & P. Werstine, Eds.). Folger Shakespeare Library. https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/othello/ (Original work published 1622)
MLAShakespeare, William. Othello. Edited by Barbara A. Mowat and Paul Werstine, Folger Shakespeare Library, folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/othello/.
  • Identity — Othello’s racial and cultural identity; outsider status in Venice.
  • Transformation — From noble general to murderer through Iago’s manipulation.
  • Perspective — How characters perceive Othello differently; the politics of seeing.
 Politics, power and justice

Race, jealousy, and manipulation as instruments of power; gendered violence.

Also: Culture, identity and community (the outsider in society)
Collected Poems 1909–1962Selected PoemsT. S. Eliot · Poetry · US/UK, 1911–1930
APAEliot, T. S. (1963). Collected poems, 1909–1962. Harcourt, Brace & World.
MLAEliot, T. S. Collected Poems, 1909–1962. Harcourt, Brace & World, 1963.
  • Identity — The fragmented modern self (Prufrock, Gerontion, the Magi).
  • Transformation — Spiritual transformation in ‘Journey of the Magi,’ ‘The Hollow Men,’ ‘Marina.’
  • Representation — Modernist representation of urban alienation and spiritual emptiness.
 Beliefs, values and education

Spiritual crisis, modernity vs. tradition, the secular and the sacred.

Also: Art, creativity and the imagination (modernist innovation)
Vintage International, trans. E. Dale SaundersThe Woman in the DunesAbe · Novel · Japan, 1962
APAAbe, K. (1991). The woman in the dunes (E. D. Saunders, Trans.). Vintage Books. (Original work published 1962)
MLAAbe, Kōbō. The Woman in the Dunes. Translated by E. Dale Saunders, Vintage Books, 1991.
  • Identity — Niki’s loss and recreation of self in captivity.
  • Transformation — From urban entomologist to dune dweller; existential reorientation.
  • Perspective — Existentialist questioning of freedom, conformity, and meaning.
 Science, technology and the environment

Sand as nature shaping and trapping humans; environment as agent of change.

Also: Politics, power and justice (the village’s authority and individual free will)
Oxford World’s ClassicsSelected Short StoriesMansfield · Short fiction · NZ/UK, 1920–1923
APAMansfield, K. (1922). The garden party and other stories. Constable. https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1429
MLAMansfield, Katherine. The Garden Party and Other Stories. Constable, 1922. Project Gutenberg, gutenberg.org/ebooks/1429.
  • Identity — The self-deceiving and constrained self (Miss Brill, the Lady’s Maid).
  • Perspective — Free indirect discourse; the sudden epiphany that reframes a life.
  • Representation — How class, gender, and ageing are rendered in modernist miniature.
 Culture, identity and community

Loneliness, class, and constrained female lives in the modern world.

Also: Politics, power and justice (gender and servitude)

Concepts: Identity, Transformation, Representation.   Field of inquiry: Beliefs, values and education — spiritual crisis, modernity against tradition, the secular and the sacred.
